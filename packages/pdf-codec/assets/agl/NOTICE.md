- Upstream URLs: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-type-tools/agl-aglfn/4036a9ca80a62f64f9de4f7321a9a045ad0ecfd6/glyphlist.txt and https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-type-tools/agl-aglfn/4036a9ca80a62f64f9de4f7321a9a045ad0ecfd6/zapfdingbats.txt
- Licence: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-type-tools/agl-aglfn/4036a9ca80a62f64f9de4f7321a9a045ad0ecfd6/LICENSE.md

`src/glyph-data/adobe-glyph-list.ts` embeds both lists' entry lines as strings, generated by `scripts/generate-glyph-list-asset.mjs` from the files here; see that script's header comment for how to regenerate it after replacing either file. `src/glyph-names.ts` resolves glyph names through them by the [Adobe Glyph List Specification](https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/agl-specification)'s algorithm.
